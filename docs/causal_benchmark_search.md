Claims searched for and not verified. No difference-in-differences, synthetic-control or event-study estimate was found for the opening of a Disney, Universal, Legoland or Merlin park, so none of those parks appears in the table. The Hong Kong government's figure of HK$148 billion of net economic benefit from Hong Kong Disneyland is a 1999 projection over 40 years at 1999 prices, with first-year attendance projected at 4.1 to 5.2 million (https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/200712/05/P200712050126_print.htm). The Singapore Minister for Trade and Industry projected about S$2.7 billion of value added, about 0.8 percent of GDP, and 30,000 jobs by 2015 from the Sentosa integrated resort before it opened (https://www.meti.gov.sg/newsroom/transcript-of-remarks-by-mr-lim-hng-kiang-at-the-press-conference-on-the-award-of-the-integrated-resort-on-sentosa--8-december-2006/); no ex post check of those figures was found. The Guggenheim Bilbao claim of nearly 4,400 jobs comes from the museum's own report and the independent estimate in the table is 1,000 to 1,200; two earlier articles by Plaza on the museum's tourism effect and return on investment could be read only as abstracts without results (https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/10780870022184859 and https://www.ijurr.org/article/the-return-on-investment-of-the-guggenheim-museum-bilbao/). A project report hosted by the Chinese University of Hong Kong economics department asks whether Shanghai Disneyland reduced Hong Kong Disneyland visits but reports no estimate (https://econ.cuhk.edu.hk/projex2017/pdf/27B.pdf). Florido-Benitez (2025) states that theme parks and museums in Asia and the Pacific boost tourism demand but the paper is descriptive and reports no model-based effect (https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s43546-024-00768-6). Zhang and Zhang (2023) estimate a difference-in-differences effect of 5A scenic area designation on city GDP per capita, which concerns designation and income rather than an opening and arrivals, so it was left out (https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/15/7/5649).
