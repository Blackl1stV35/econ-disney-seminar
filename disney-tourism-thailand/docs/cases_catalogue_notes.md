# Cases catalogue notes

The catalogue has 69 cases in 32 economies: 30 theme parks, 23 integrated resorts, 6 multi-park resorts, 5 destination resorts and 5 mega attractions.

Evidence grade: A 20 rows, B 28 rows, C 21 rows. Grade A needs an operator, government or annual report page that confirms the opening month and, where a USD cost is given, the cost. Grade B needs two news or press release pages. Grade C rests on one secondary page or an encyclopedia.

Panel feasible: 54 rows have panel_feasible = Y (opening year 2000 to 2018, economy in the 96-economy panel). 12 cases opened before 2000 and 3 after 2018.

Rows where sources conflict:
- Date or which event counts as the opening (8): CHN_2014_chimelong_ocean_kingdom, ARE_2008_atlantis_the_palm, ARE_2018_warner_bros_world_abu_dhabi, SGP_2010_resorts_world_sentosa, ESP_1995_portaventura, ESP_1997_guggenheim_bilbao, ESP_2000_terra_mitica, ZAF_2000_montecasino.

Fields left blank:
- investment_usd_bn_nominal and investment_year_basis, 48 rows: a dollar figure is entered only when two news pages or an operator, government or annual report page give a dated figure; other figures stay in investment_source_note.
- first_year_attendance_m, 61 rows: entered only where a page states an opening-year count.
- operator, 1 row: RUS_2014_sochi_park.
- concurrent_confounds, 30 rows: no event from the year before to the year after opening was documented from a fetched page.
- other_openings_same_economy_within_5y, 24 rows: no other catalogue case in that economy within five years.

Conventions: opening_date is the first opening to the general public, to the day where a page states it and to the month otherwise (8 rows); soft, official and grand openings are in notes. investment_year_basis is the year of the cited figure.
